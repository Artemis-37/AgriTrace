# /hook Command
Triggers the hormozi-hook-writer skill.
When this command is used, immediately ask:
1. What is your product? (PDF guide, template, course, etc.)
2. Who is your exact avatar?
3. What specific result does it deliver in what timeframe?
4. What awareness level is your target prospect?
Then generate 10 hooks using the full Hormozi hook system.
