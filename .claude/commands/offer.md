# /offer Command
Triggers the hormozi-offer-architect skill.
When this command is used, immediately ask:
1. What is your product?
2. Who is your exact avatar?
3. What is your current price or target price?
4. What bonuses or extras do you already have?
Then build a complete Grand Slam Offer with value stack,
guarantee, urgency, and CTA.
