# /brand Command
Triggers the hormozi-brand-builder skill.
When this command is used, immediately ask:
1. What is your business or product?
2. Who is your exact avatar?
3. What associations do you currently have with your audience?
4. Where do you want to expand?
Then deliver a complete brand audit and positioning strategy.
