# Hormozi Hook Writer
## Core Principle
"If someone doesn't make it through the hook, then nothing else matters."
Prep-time rule: 80% hooks / 20% meat / 0% CTAs.
Record 10 hook variations for every 1 piece of content.
Assembly formula: 50 Hooks × 3-5 Meats × 1-3 CTAs = 150-750 unique ads per week.
## The Cocktail Party Effect
"Imagine you're at a club. Lots of people are hooting and hollering. Loud
music thumping. In all that noise, a single sound pierces through it all
and you turn around. Wanna know the sound? Your name. You hear it, and
instantly look for the source. Scientists call it the cocktail party effect.
In simple terms, even when there's tons of stuff going on, a single thing
can still catch and hold our attention. So our goal with our call outs is
to harness the cocktail party effect and cut through all the noise."
## The 2-Part Hook Framework
Part 1 — THE CALL OUT: gets the prospect to say "This is for me"
Part 2 — CONDITION FOR VALUE: "If [TARGET] [ACTION] they will get [REWARD]"
Word-for-word template:
"If [MOMS - target] [WATCH THIS VIDEO - condition]
[THEY WILL KNOW HOW TO GET THEIR KIDS TO BEHAVE - reward]"
## The 8 Verbal Hook Types
### 1. LABELS — words the avatar identifies with
"Local business owners, I have a gift for you"
"Chiropractors I have a gift for you"
Apply for digital products:
"Working moms going back to work — this is for you"
"Freelancers who want their first $5k month — read this"
### 2. QUESTIONS
Yes questions: "Would you pay $1,000 dollars to have the business of
your dreams in 30 days?"
Comparison: "Would you rather struggle to get patients for the rest of
your life or fix it in 6 weeks?"
Open: "Which would you rather be?"
Apply: "Would you pay $27 to plan your full week in 22 minutes?"
### 3. CONDITIONALS — scenarios leading to a result
"If you're working all the time and your business isn't growing,
you're working on the wrong sh*t"
"If you're a chiropractor, this video will get you more leads"
Apply: "If you're a working mom and Sundays feel like dread —
this changes everything"
### 4. COMMANDS — direct suggestions
"Read this if you're tired of being broke"
"Watch this if you want to get more patients"
Apply: "Download this if you want your first client in 14 days"
### 5. STATEMENTS — bold claims or facts
"The smartest thing you can do today…"
"How to get ahead of 99% of people"
"The top 1% of chiropractor clinics follow these rules…"
Apply: "The top 1% of working moms all do this one thing on Sunday"
### 6. LISTS OR STEPS — listicles
"In this video I'm going to talk to you about the 28 ways to stay poor"
"11 ways Chiropractors get more patients without paid ads"
Apply: "7 things successful moms do before 9am (free printable checklist)"
### 7. NARRATIVES — stories and anecdotes
"One day I was in the back and this old lady comes in and she was piss angry"
"All of a sudden, my phone starts ringing off the hook…"
Apply: "One day I looked at my planner and realized I hadn't completed
a single task in 3 weeks..."
### 8. EXCLAMATIONS / PROVOCATIVE STATEMENTS
"Ahhhhh… This is the blueprint to becoming a millionaire and I'm going
to walk you through the levels"
Apply: "This $27 PDF changed how 3,000 moms plan their entire week"
## The 70-20-10 Hook Innovation Rule
### 70% CORE — more of what works
Use hooks already proven in your own content or other people's content.
Example: Using best-performing posts on X as hooks for video shorts.
Proven structure: "[Result] in [timeframe] — even if [objection]"
Apply: "Plan your week in 22 minutes — even if you've failed at planners before"
Apply: "Land your first client in 7 days — even if your portfolio is empty"
Rule: Let the data do the teaching. Look at top 10% of content,
analyze those hooks, make more like them. Bottom 10% — make less.
### 20% EMERGING — winner adjacent
Find something working in another niche, model the concept for your business.
Use software to find outliers on a platform and repackage the formula.
Permutate a winner: change format (list→question), emotion (hope→fear),
or avatar (moms→single dads).
### 10% BIG NEW IDEAS — risky but high upside
Totally new visual formats and hooks that might fail but could be huge wins.
Example: Catching a banana at the beginning of an ad.
When one wins → it becomes the new 70%.
## Awareness Levels — Match Hook to Prospect State
MOST AWARE → Offer-driven
Template: "50% off our best-selling XYZ product for the rest of the month"
B2C example: "XFast's new formula: now with 25% more protein - Same great taste!"
B2B example: "Digitalboost: now offering social media management at 20% off"
PRODUCT-AWARE → Proof-driven
Template: "Discover why 10,000 people chose XYZ to solve their [problem] last month"
B2C: "Why 9 out of 10 XFast users reached their goal weight within 3 months"
B2B: "See how Digitalboost increased ROI by 150% for 5 different industries"
SOLUTION-AWARE → Promise-driven
Template: "The fastest way to [achieve desired result] — introducing XYZ"
B2C: "Lose 15 pounds in 30 days with our scientifically proven system"
B2B: "Double your online sales in 6 months with our data-driven strategies"
PROBLEM-AWARE → Pain-driven
Template: "Tired of [specific problem]? There's a better way"
B2C: "Frustrated with crash diets that don't last? There's a sustainable way"
B2B: "Is your website getting sales? You might be missing these crucial elements"
COMPLETELY UNAWARE → Curiosity-driven
Template: "The hidden danger in your daily routine that's costing you [money/time/health]"
B2C: "The hidden hormonal imbalance that's making 1 in 3 Americans gain weight"
B2B: "The unexpected way your business is losing $1000s each month"
