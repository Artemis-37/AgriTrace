# Hormozi Offer Architect
## The Grand Slam Offer Result
Same $10,000 ad spend comparison:
- Commodity offer: 40 appointments → 5 sales at $1,000 = $5,000 collected
- Grand Slam Offer: 100 appointments → 28 sales at $3,997 = $112,000 collected
Result: 22.4x more cash for the same ad spend.
## Step 1 — Avatar 4-Point Test
All 4 must be YES before building anything:
GROWING: "If I have to pick between an audience that's shrinking, one
that's maintaining, and one that's growing... I'd rather go after one
that's bigger. Because even if I stay the same size, I'm still going to
grow because the market's growing."
PURCHASING POWER: "I want to make sure they can afford my stuff... if I
want to charge a lot of money, they've got to have the money so I can
charge it."
EASY TO TARGET: "I'd rather have customers I can put advertisements in
front of with little effort."
IN PAIN: "I want people who desperately want what I have to sell...
I'd rather make it easy."
## Step 2 — The Value Stack Formula
Stack until prospect feels "stupid saying no."
Minimum 5:1 ratio: total perceived value vs price.
Example math for a $27 digital product:
Main PDF guide .......................... $37 value
Bonus 1: printable checklist ............. $17 value
Bonus 2: swipe file / templates .......... $27 value
Bonus 3: video walkthrough ............... $47 value
Bonus 4: bonus PDF ....................... $17 value
Bonus 5: community access ................ $27 value
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total perceived value: $172
Your price today: $27 → Ratio: 6.4:1 ✓
## Step 3 — Bonus Types (Hormozi Exact)
BONUS UNLOCKS: "Create high-value items (trainings, scripts, or status
tiers) that are for sale separately but can be unlocked for free if a
customer performs a specific action, such as submitting a testimonial."
AWARD STATUS FRAMEWORK: "Create an aspirational goal and tie it to a
physical award associated with prestige." One award = 4 proof types:
written community post + 6-point video + unboxing video + on-stage recording.
ENGAGEMENT UNLOCKS: "Incentivize product use by unlocking courses, 1-on-1
calls, or event tickets as customers reach specific activity levels."
STATUS TIERS: "Custom badges or profile indicators to show a member is
'in' once they achieve a milestone."
## Step 4 — Pricing Strategies (Exact Numbers)
BIG HEAD LONG TAIL MODEL:
"Charge a high one-time fee for education or setup, followed by a small
recurring fee for community or access."
Example: $6,800 upfront + $199/month recurring.
For digital products: $27 one-time + $7/month community.
LAUNCH PRICE STRATEGY:
Set real price at 2x the launch price.
Example: $47 normally → $27 launch with hard deadline.
After launch: remove bonuses, restore full price. Never extend deadline.
ANNUAL PRICING LOGIC:
"Buy 10 months, get 2 free" = 16% off.
Rule: if average stay > 12 months → use this strategy.
FOUNDER RATES:
"50% discount for early adopters to incentivize them to stay forever
and build the initial community."
PREMIUM PRICING POWER:
Strong brand = charge 2x, 5x, or 10x more for same product.
Example: Starbucks charges $5 for $1 coffee through brand association.
## Step 5 — Guarantee Formulas (Word for Word)
OUTCOME GUARANTEE:
"I f&cking guarantee you that you will be making more than people who
have 4-year degrees. You'll probably start making more by the end of
your first year."
THE REDO (Cancellation Save):
"Let me give it another shot and I'll make it right."
THE VALIDATION SAVE:
"That's completely ridiculous. You're 100% correct. Will you give me
the opportunity to make it right?"
THE UPSELL SAVE:
"You should have been sold into that anyways. And that's on me."
(Move churning customer to higher-tier program.)
Rule: The stronger the guarantee, the higher the conversion AND the
lower the refund rate.
## Step 6 — Urgency Types
TIME-BASED: "Before it expires" — hard calendar deadline.
"Launch pricing ends Tuesday March 31st at midnight EST"
OFFER-DRIVEN: "50% off our best-selling XYZ product for the rest of the month"
ASSET-LOSS SCARCITY: Remind customers what they lose if they cancel:
custom URLs, posts, community access, Founder Pricing.
MILESTONE URGENCY: "I won't even charge you a penny to help you build
a business in the next 30 days"
## Step 7 — Offer Naming (By Awareness Level)
Most Aware: "50% off our best-selling XYZ product"
Product-Aware: "Discover why 10,000 people chose XYZ last month"
Solution-Aware: "The fastest way to [result] — introducing XYZ"
Problem-Aware: "Tired of [problem]? There's a better way"
Unaware: "The hidden danger in your daily routine costing you [money]"
General formula: "[Adjective] [Result] [Format] for [Avatar]"
RIGHT: "The Working Mom's 22-Minute Weekly Planner System"
RIGHT: "The 14-Day First Client Blueprint for Freelancers"
## Step 8 — CTA Formula (Word for Word)
"A good CTA shows or tells: what to do, how to do it, when to do it,
what they get for doing it, and what happens next."
What to do: "Take advantage of this great offer by..."
How to do it: "...tapping the button on the bottom of your screen..."
When to do it: "...before it expires..."
What they get: "...and you'll get $1000 of free stuff..."
What happens next: "...delivered straight to your inbox."
SHOW AND TELL: Beyond verbalizing, visually demonstrate the next steps —
show the exact screen, form, and submit button the user will see.
