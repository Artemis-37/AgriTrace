# Hormozi Brand Builder
## Branding Definition
"Branding is the deliberate pairing of a business with associations that
a majority of the ideal audience likes."
## The 3 Measurement Variables
REACH: "The output of advertising — the amount of people who know about
my stuff. A brand cannot exist without reach, making it the primary element."
INFLUENCE: "The chance it changes behavior when they see it."
DIRECTION: "How their behavior changes — towards or away. This depends
on the target audience, as one association may cause a towards reaction
in one group and an away reaction in another."
## The 8 Brand Positions Matrix
High Reach + Low Influence + Towards:
"Taylor Swift. In general, I think she's good but probably won't buy
much of her stuff."
High Reach + High Influence + Towards:
"Elon Musk. I own two Teslas. Use Starlink... and in general support
whatever he does because I think he's good for the world."
High Reach + High Influence + Away:
"Terrorists. I am strongly opposed to them."
High Reach + Low Influence + Away:
"Hollywood actors. In general, I find them whiny and entitled."
Low Reach + High Influence + Towards:
"My close friends. They don't have big reach, but they have stronger
influence over me."
Low Reach + Low Influence + Away:
"Stranger in the street. Don't have much influence, but in general,
I avoid them."
Low Reach + High Influence + Away:
"Copycats and scammers. Most people haven't heard of them. But I have
strong influence away from them."
Low Reach + Low Influence + Towards:
"Acquaintances. Not many people know them. But in general, I'm inclined
to support them."
## Avatar Selection — 4-Point Criteria
GROWING: "If I have to pick between an audience that's shrinking, one
that's maintaining, and one that's growing... I'd rather go after one
that's bigger. Because even if I stay the same size, I'm still going to
grow because the market's growing."
PURCHASING POWER: "I want to make sure they can afford my stuff."
EASY TO TARGET: "I'd rather have customers I can put advertisements in
front of with little effort."
IN PAIN: "I want people who desperately want what I have to sell."
## The 4-Step Branding Framework
Step 1: "Figure out who your ideal customer is."
Step 2: "Figure out what they like."
Step 3: "Associate your stuff with those things."
Step 4: "Optimize those associations for max profit."
## Levels of Authority Cycle
WHAT WE SAY — least influential:
"This is me telling you, advertising myself, making my stuff known.
Content, Ads, Outreach."
WHAT OTHERS SAY — more influential:
"What other people say to them matters way more than what you tell them.
Affiliates, and other customers."
WHAT THEY EXPERIENCE — most influential:
"A person's own opinion of their experience has, by far, the most
authority. This determines what they tell others and governs
advertisements over the long haul."
THE VIRTUOUS CYCLE: Advertise successfully + deliver on promises →
"forces strong positive associations" → they buy again + tell others.
## The Bouquet Metaphor
"I think about branding like assembling a bouquet of flowers. On their
own, flowers are not a bouquet, just as products, values, experiences,
people, etc., on their own are not a brand. And just as tying many
flowers together forms a bouquet, so too does tying products, values,
experiences, and so on form a brand."
## The 5 Brand Expansion Directions
UP MARKET: "Larger customers. Ex: Multi-location assisted living owners."
DOWN MARKET: "Smaller customers. Ex: At-home care and mobile assisted living."
ADJACENT: "Similar market, but slightly different. Ex: rehab facilities."
BROADER: "The entire category. Ex: all senior living centers."
NARROWER: "A slice of your existing market. Ex: Assisted living with 100-500 residents."
## Branding Mistake Recovery Technique
"One bad pairing can absolutely hurt a brand, much like one ugly flower
messes up the whole bouquet."
Recovery: "You just have to overwhelm customers with the stuff they like
until eventually the bad pairing shrinks into irrelevance. We don't try
and eliminate the DUI, we don't try and eliminate the dead rose. It happened;
there's nothing we can do about it. But what we try to do is to just
overwhelm it with way more of the stuff that the majority of our people
actually like."
