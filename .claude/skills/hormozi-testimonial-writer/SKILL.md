# Hormozi Testimonial Writer
## The Belief Continuum
"Belief is not an all-or-nothing binary; it is a measure of how much a
prospect believes." Prospects start at a point of doubt. "The role of
marketing and sales is to get people as close to absolute certainty as
possible. The closer your proof gets prospects to absolute certainty,
the more compelling the proof."
100% DOUBT ←————————————————→ 100% CERTAINTY
Your proof is your only true differentiator — promises can be copied,
proof cannot.
## The 13-Factor Proof Hierarchy (Strongest to Weakest)
1. In-Person > Virtual: "People believe what they see with their own
   eyes more than a digital version."
2. Live > Recorded: "A customer walking on stage live is more compelling
   than a video of the same testimonial."
3. Raw > Processed: "Unedited moments are more believable than highly
   produced content."
4. Show > Tell: "Showing a packed gym or a video spliced together is
   better than simply describing results."
5. Other People > You: "You are the most biased person in existence;
   others' opinions carry more authority."
6. Identical to Them > Opposite: "Prospects must think 'this is me'
   when they see the proof."
7. Personal > Generic: "Specific pains, like 'thighs would chafe,'
   outperform generic statements like 'I was fat'."
8. Big Results > Small Results > No Results: "Larger transformations
   are more compelling."
9. Newer Proof > Older Proof: "Constant updates prevent prospects from
   assuming you haven't had a good result since."
10. More Proof > Less Proof: "Conquering with sheer numbers creates a
    floor-to-ceiling population-wide attitude of success."
11. Third Party Verification > Zero Verification: "Using industry awards,
    certifications, or trusted sites like Amazon builds credible endorsement."
12. Proof With Numbers > Proof Without Numbers: "People have a hard time
    arguing with understandable numbers visualized in charts and graphs."
13. Metaphors > Technical Jargon: "Comparing technical components to things
    the audience already understands is incredibly persuasive."
## The 6-Point Testimonial Script (Word for Word)
Point 1 — INTERNAL STRUGGLE:
"What was rock bottom before doing this? What did you have to do that
you hated? What things did you want to do that you couldn't?"
Point 2 — EXTERNAL STRUGGLE:
"What were your objective metrics?"
Point 3 — SKEPTICISM:
"What were your main concerns about buying/clicking/joining?"
Point 4 — OVERCOME:
"What made you do it anyways?"
Point 5 — EXTERNAL VICTORY:
"What are your objective metrics now?"
Point 6 — INTERNAL VICTORY:
"What's been the best moment since? What can you do now you couldn't
do before? What things did you hate doing you don't have to do anymore?"
## UGC Collection Methods (9 Nodes)
1. LIFECYCLE ADS: "Record all calls (sales, onboarding, support,
   ascension) to clip 'before' moments from sales calls, 'during' from
   support, and 'after' from award calls. Cut together into one narrative."
2. UPON DELIVERY ADS: "Capture raw reaction moments, like a ribbon
   cutting, when a client first sees their results."
3. SOCIAL MEDIA SCRAPE: "Establish a community hashtag and set reminders
   to save tagged media and disappearing stories across all platforms."
4. COMMUNICATION SCRAPE: "Have support reps search internal chats for
   win terms like 'Sales, $, PIF, Profit' and screenshot the messages."
5. COMMUNITY SCRAPE: "Run a win of the week post every Friday and
   screen-record the scroll of comments."
6. REVIEW SITE SCRAPE: "Systematically sort sites like Google, Yelp,
   and Amazon by newest to capture fresh screenshots."
7. BONUS UNLOCK METHOD: "Create a high-value giveaway normally for sale
   separately. Tell customers they can buy it — or unlock it for free by
   submitting a testimonial video hitting the 6-point script."
8. TESTIMONIAL COMPETITION: "Run a raw iPhone-style competition once or
   twice a year. Customers compete for a major prize. Winner determined
   by which testimonial performs best as a paid ad based on conversions
   over 30 days. Use the 6-point script as submission requirement."
9. IN-PERSON EVENT TECHNIQUES:
   Stage Ads: "Photos or videos of founder on stage with celebrating crowd."
   Professional Testimonials: "Dedicated camera team in a side room to
   interview top clients using the 6-point script."
   Man on the Street: "Rapid-fire interviews during breaks asking a single
   question to get 10+ responses for highlight reels."
## Award Status Framework
"Create an aspirational goal for customers and bestow a physical award
associated with status, like a sword and shield."
One award generates 4 proof types:
- A written results post in the community
- A 6-point video testimonial
- An unboxing video of the award
- An on-stage recording of the presentation
